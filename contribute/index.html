We have no description, website or Instagram for 173 clubs (146 clubs we know only by name). We searched club sites, Harvard pages and the web and couldn't find anything we could confirm. Students who know these clubs can fill the gap in a minute.

Run a club? Update your club's page.

Missing a club entirely? Tell us.

How this works
  • Add info opens a short form with the club already filled in. Answer only what you know: what it does, its website or Instagram, how to join.
  • A person checks every answer before it appears, and it's shown as student-reported.
  • A link to where you saw it helps us confirm it, but it isn't required.
  • Please don't include anyone's name, email or phone number, yours or a member's.
