About

At Harvard Undergraduate Kaizen Club, we believe that the most rewarding paths are often the most difficult. Our club thrives on the philosophy that consistent, marginal improvements can lead to profound growth. We’re not just about achieving goals; we’re about embracing the process, pushing our own limitations, and finding fulfillment in the challenges we choose to face.
