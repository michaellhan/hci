The Harvard Undergraduate Council

Politics & Policy

No longer active

Elected undergraduate body organized into standing committees that advocate for, fund, and support the student body.

No longer active. The Harvard Undergraduate Council was replaced by Harvard Undergraduate Association in 2022. This page is kept for reference.

Source: en.wikipedia.org

  • harvarduc.org

About

The 51 Members of the Harvard Undergraduate Council are sorted amongst a number of standing committees, each with a designated area of oversight. Learn more about what these committees do and who staffs them.

What they do
  • Funding student club events through Club Grants and Grants for an Open Harvard College
  • General meetings open to the public
  • Lyft "Essential Ride" Partnership offering students Lyft ride credit
  • Advocacy on academic concerns such as grading policy and tuition costs
  • Advocacy for mental health resources and feminine hygiene product accessibility
  • Club recognition process through the Rules Committee
  • Spring 2021 Student Experience Survey
  • Movies for Mental Health workshop

Mission, in their words

Advocate for and support the undergraduate body in service to the student body.
