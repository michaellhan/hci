About

Established in the fall of 1981, the Harvard College Debating Union (HCDU) is a student-run organization at Harvard College and is Harvard's only internationally competitive debate association. The HCDU consistently ranks as one of the top three debate teams in the country. We were formerly known as the Harvard Speech and Parliamentary Debate Society (HSPDS).

How to join
  • Application — A written application, often followed by interviews.
  • Comp — Short for “competition”: a trial period of a few weeks where you learn and do the work, then the club decides. Some comps take everyone who finishes. More
  • Tryout — You try out in practice or a skills session.

What they do
  • Running Harvard's three yearly debate tournaments
  • Weekly general meetings, practice rounds, and casing sessions
  • Competing in American and British Parliamentary debate tournaments
  • Regular socials, team dinners, and a yearly off-campus retreat
  • Domestic and international travel to tournaments
  • Hosting the Harvard College Debating Union Season Opener high school tournament
  • Hosting the Harvard College World Schools Invitational
  • Hosting a fall APDA tournament

Mission, in their words

To be a community of equal access, free of discrimination, prejudice, or bias.
