About

Rejection gave birth to the Asian American Association. During first-year orientation week in 1976, two Asian American women decided to attend a Minority Freshman Banquet sponsored by the university, but were barred from entering and were turned away.

Mission, in their words

Our mission AAA strives to unite Asian American students at Harvard and raise greater awareness of Asian American issues through social, cultural, and political events. We aim to FOSTER a community; PROVIDE a forum for Asian Americans at Harvard; ADDRESS political, economic, and social issues; CELEBRATE achievements of Asian Americans; DEVELOP long-lasting partnerships; and INSPIRE participants to contemplate their roles and responsibilities in forging the future of Asian America.
