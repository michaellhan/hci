About

AI Safety Student Team supports undergraduate and graduate students in conducting research to reduce risks from advanced AI. Opportunities include a semester-long introductory reading group on AI safety topics and a Governance, Policy & Strategy Fellowship.

What they do
  • Governance, Policy & Strategy Fellowship
  • Semester-long introductory reading group on AI safety topics
  • Research to reduce risks from advanced AI
  • Introductory Technical AI Safety Fellowship (9-week reading group)
  • Weekly member meetings discussing alignment research and policy proposals
  • Internal talks and Q&As by alignment/governance researchers and professors
  • Semesterly workshops
  • Joint AISST and MAIA workshops

Mission, in their words

To reduce catastrophic risks from AI through research, education, and capacity building.
