About

Our flagship product is Harvard’s first open data catalog, where we’ve gathered dozens of public Harvard datasets in a free repository. We’ve used data science and data visualization techniques to analyze data on campus safety, faculty gender disparity, student government election turnout, and trends in concentration popularity, to name a few.

What they do
  • Maintains Harvard's first open data catalog, a free repository of public Harvard datasets
  • Data science and data visualization analyses of campus data, such as campus safety, faculty gender disparity, student government election turnout, and concentration popularity trends
  • Data Catalog: searchable repository of public university datasets
  • Projects: written and interactive data analyses
  • Nonprofit partnerships
  • Survey Group
