About

The Leadership Institute at Harvard College (LIHC) is a student organization devoted to fostering the awareness, skills, and values of leadership among Harvard undergraduates. By providing skill-building programs and channels for collaboration between student leaders, LIHC aims to inspire and empower students to fulfill their leadership potential at Harvard, in their communities and in their world.

What they do
  • Leadership Development Initiative: skill-building workshops and conversations with premier leaders
  • The Presidents Forum: discussion forums connecting student leaders on campus
  • Social Outreach: teaching leadership in an after-school program for low-income middle school students with Citizen Schools
  • Harvard Undergraduate Leadership Magazine: annual leadership magazine
  • External Relations: strategic and financial backbone; main partner with the Ivy Council
  • Serving as counselors and mentors for the YOUth Lead the Change teen leadership conference
  • Leadership blog posts
  • Members served as counselors at the YOUth Lead the Change conference for teenagers
