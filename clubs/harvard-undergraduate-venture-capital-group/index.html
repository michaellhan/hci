About

The Harvard Undergraduate Venture Capital Group's core mission is to drive innovation by connecting founders with investors. We do so by offering our members the ability to identify promising founders, whose startups serve as successful investments for our Partner VCs.

What they do
  • Monthly socials
  • Exclusive recruiting events with firms
  • Meeting with founders, completing investment memos, and receiving feedback from partner VCs
  • Big-Little mentorship program for new members
  • Entrepreneurship Summit (flagship event)
  • Networking events for members and the Harvard community
  • Delivering reports to VC partners
  • Training members as sourcing analysts and referring promising startups to partner VC firms

Mission, in their words

The Harvard Undergraduate Venture Capital Group (VCG) is excited to announce our second over High School Pitch Competition! At VCG, our mission is to bridge the gap between ambitious entrepreneurs and the world of venture capital. Now, we’re extending that vision to the next generation of innovators.
