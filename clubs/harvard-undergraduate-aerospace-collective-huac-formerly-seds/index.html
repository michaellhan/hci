About

The Harvard Undergraduate Aerospace Collective (HUAC) brings together engineers, scientists, and enthusiasts passionate about space and aspiring to careers in the broader aerospace industry. Officially established in early 2026, HUAC has operated under various names since 2013 and now comprises three technical teams: Satellite, Rocketry, and UAV. HUAC is a chartered chapter of Students for the Exploration and Development of Space (SEDS). Team members meet weekly for project work and gather club-wide for professional development events, including talks with industry leaders and alumni, technical workshops, and community-building activities across campus.
