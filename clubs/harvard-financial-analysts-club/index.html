About

Dedicated to providing the Harvard student body with sound financial education programs and real-world investment experience. Through the HFAC comp, new members are given a ground-up introduction to finance with a focus on internship/career preparation.

How to join
  • Comp — Short for “competition”: a trial period of a few weeks where you learn and do the work, then the club decides. Some comps take everyone who finishes. More

Only for some roles: application for running the club's fund. General membership doesn't need it.

What they do

  • Traditional Finance Comp lecture series

  • HFAC Comp Stock Pitch Competition
  • Comp socials, poker tournaments, recruiting workshops, and board meet & greets
