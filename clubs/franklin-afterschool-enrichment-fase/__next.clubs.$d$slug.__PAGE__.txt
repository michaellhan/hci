Throughout the academic year, we aim to empower our students by reinforcing their academic skills through small-group tutoring, providing them with mentoring relationships, and creating opportunities for students to strengthen community bonds with their peers in a safe environment. Our grounding principles center restorative justice and socio-emotional learning in our curriculum, field trips, and day-to-day interactions. Every day at FASE, we make an intentional effort to create space for joy, play, and fun as we grow closer as a community.
