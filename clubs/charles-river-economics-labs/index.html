About

We are a group of Harvard, Stanford, Brown, and UChicago undergraduate researchers who conduct pro bono economic policy research for governments, intergovernmental organizations, and policy think tanks. We are supported by an annual cohort of PhD candidates, professors of economics, and industry professionals.

What they do
  • Small-group dinners with professors on the Advisory Board
  • Internal talks with industry professionals
  • One-on-one mentorship and training
  • Hosted speaker events
  • Pro bono economic policy research for governments, intergovernmental organizations, and policy think tanks
  • Regular meetings with PhD Fellows and Board of Advisors for technical advice
  • Biannual newsletter with published research
