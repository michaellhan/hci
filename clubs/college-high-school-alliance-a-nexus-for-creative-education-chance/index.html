About

CHANCE serves local high schoolers from Cambridge Rindge & Latin every Tuesday and Thursday from 3:30 to 5 right inside Harvard Yard at Phillips Brooks House. Our student tutors work with high schoolers, helping them with homework, general advice, SAT prep, and college essays.

Mission, in their words

A college preparatory program that provides SAT tutoring, homework help, and college advice, and strives to give students from Cambridge's only public high school the desire, support, and motivation to gain admission to college and succeed there and beyond.
