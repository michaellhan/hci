Radcliffe Pitches

A cappella

Gender-inclusive treble a cappella ensemble of 12-15 students singing jazz standards at Harvard and on tour, with auditions open to all Harvard students.

About

We hold a number of yearly big concerts (including the CS50 Welcome, Breast Cancer Awareness Benefit Concert; Fall Jam; Spring Jam; Harvard-Yale Jam; Freshman Jam), as well as many other on-campus and New England area performances.

How to join
  • Audition — You perform for the group, often with a callback round.

What they do
  • Hired performances at private and public events
  • Charity fundraisers and benefit concerts
  • Annual performance for the Hasty Pudding Man of the Year
  • Performing jazz a cappella for public and private audiences, including international tours
  • Annual spring trip to Bermuda for performances and benefit concerts
  • Performances at events such as galas, weddings, charity fundraisers, and corporate conferences
  • Yearly concerts in Cambridge including Fall Jam, Spring Jam, Harvard-Yale Jam, and others
  • Annual concerts including Fall Jam, Spring Jam, Harvard-Yale Jam, Freshman Jam, CS50 Welcome, and Breast Cancer Awareness Benefit Concert
