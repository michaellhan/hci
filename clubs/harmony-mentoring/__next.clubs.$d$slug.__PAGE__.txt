We welcome instrumentalists and vocalists from all backgrounds to join our community.HARMONY volunteers dedicate at least one hour each week to providing the highest quality instruction to their mentees. During weekly meetings, volunteers often begin with technique and review, before moving into new material. Music education has a boundless impact on the Cambridge community, both now and in the future.
