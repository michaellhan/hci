About

Harvard Yearbook Publications traces its origins back to the Harvard Portfolio, first published in 1889. Over the decades, the publication evolved through several iterations, including the Harvard Album, which was eventually superseded in 1949 by Harvard Yearbook Publications.

What they do
  • Producing the senior Harvard Yearbook
  • Producing the Freshman Register (First Year Register)
  • Senior portrait photosittings
  • Layout, Business, and Photo boards
  • Layout Board: produce the 520-page Harvard Yearbook and 128-page Freshman Register using Adobe InDesign and Photoshop
  • Business Board: financial operations, marketing strategy, and advertising sales
  • Photo Board: event photography and visual storytelling

Mission, in their words

To provide a permanent, beautiful record of the four years spent at Harvard and a warm, accurate welcome in print for each new class.
