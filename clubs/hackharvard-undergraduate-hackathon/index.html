About

HackHarvard is a 36-hour undergraduate hackathon hosted at Harvard University. Every year, over 500 students from around the world come to our campus to build, learn, and create.

  • HackHarvard China Challenge in Hangzhou with pre-event workshops and a 36-hour hackathon
  • Annual 36-hour hackathon where students build software, hardware, art, tools, and experiments
