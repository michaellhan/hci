About

The Harvard Law School Blockchain & FinTech Initiative works to deepen students’ understanding of blockchain technology, the fintech ecosystem, and the legal, economic, social, and political challenges and opportunities these technologies present.

What they do
  • Practitioner-led workshops
  • Annual Conference
  • Lunch panels
  • Networking and career development (small-group dinners, coffee chats, mentorship pairings, recruitment pathways)
  • Newsletter

Mission, in their words

We aim to connect and prepare HLS students for careers in the fintech and blockchain industry. We are a vibrant community of students, professionals, and faculty at Harvard.
