About

The Harvard Chess Club (HCC) is dedicated to promoting the strategic game of chess and fostering a community of players from all backgrounds and skill levels. Our mission is to provide opportunities for members to practice their gameplay, enhance their knowledge, and engage in professional opportunities. Harvard Chess Club, 2026.
