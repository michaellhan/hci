About

Founded in 2005, the goal of the Harvard College Pan-African Dance and Music Ensemble is to provide a space for the creative expression of various forms of dance from the African continent. The company provides a combination of dancing and live drumming in rehearsals and at performances. Dances performed are choreographed by both students and a professional choreographer. We perform within the Harvard community and also within the greater Boston community in order to expose others to African culture.
