About

Founded by two marketing enthusiasts at Harvard, HUMG redefines student-led marketing through collaborative innovation. We replace hierarchy with peer-driven strategy — harnessing campus insights, agile frameworks, and collective ideation to build campaigns that resonate. Here, marketing expertise grows from shared learning, not textbooks; futures are shaped by equal voices, not handed-down formulas. This is academia meeting action, democratized.

Mission, in their words

Why do some businesses stand out on a crowded Harvard Square? It’s more than just location – it’s the way they connect with today’s dynamic, fast-moving generation. Look around, and you’ll see students making mindful choices, fueled by individuality. Our mission is to help businesses understand and engage Gen Z, crafting marketing strategies that captivate, resonate, and drive loyalty.
