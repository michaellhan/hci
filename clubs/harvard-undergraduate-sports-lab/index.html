Mission, in their words

Secure your ticket for a chance to hear from the top leaders in the Sports Business Industry! The Harvard Undergraduate Sports Lab (HUSL) seeks to create and empower a community of students who are seeking pathways into the Sports Business Industry. With our events, our goal is to build one of the most diverse and influential sports communities in the world. Stay tuned for more information on our speakers. As a sneak peak, we will be hearing from executives at the CAA, Red Sox, Fenway Sports Group, Clearlake Capital, Unrivaled, U.S. Soccer Federation, RedBird, and More!
