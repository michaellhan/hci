About

It’s not an accident that the Band has engaged so many members for such a long time, and we’re eager for you to learn what makes the Band so special — to its members, to Harvard University, and to the Cambridge, Boston and New England communities.

How to join
  • Open membership — Anyone can join: sign up or come to a meeting.
  • Audition — You perform for the group, often with a callback round.

Sources disagree: one says anyone can join, another describes a selection process. Check the club's own page for which applies to you.
