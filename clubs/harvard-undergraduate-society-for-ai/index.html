About

“This group is dedicated to exploring, discussing, and implementing the cutting edge of intelligence advancements and their impacts on society. In doing so, it aims to be the launching place for anyone interested in engaging in AI as part of the broader mission of Harvard Computer Society, whether from a perspective, a policy angle, or interdisciplinary approaches.”
