About

From picking courses and choosing a major to finding research opportunities and exploring extracurriculars, being a pre-med student requires substantial commitment and good long-term planning. Luckily, Harvard has many resources and organizations available that enable students to navigate these waters with the help and expertise of experienced advisors, faculty members, and other students. Among these resources are the Office of Career Services (which provides 1-on-1 advising for all aspects of pre-health, as well as detailed information regarding course planning), URAF (Undergraduate Research and Fellowships), concentration advisers, house tutors, Pre-Med Peer Liaisons (PPL PAFs) and more. Click the links below to check out some of these great resources at Harvard and make sure to join our mailing list for information about events and more organized by these programs.
