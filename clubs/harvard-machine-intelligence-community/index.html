About

The Harvard Undergraduate Machine Intelligence Community (HUMIC) is the primary space for undergraduates interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning to gather, socialize, and learn from one another. We serve the unique role of hosting completely undergraduate-run workshops and projects exploring state-of-the-art developments.
