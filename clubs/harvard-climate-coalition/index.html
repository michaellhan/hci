About

This coalition aims to unify the Harvard undergraduate climate space to create an overarching and diverse climate community on campus. We seek to spark and deepen relationships that spur impactful climate collaborations. We believe that diversity—of thought, identity, geography, and experience— and collaboration are our greatest strengths.

What they do
  • Documentary screening and Q&A
  • Our Climate Change Stories storytelling event
  • Intersectional Earth Week
  • Climate mixer (HUKEG) with HUCEG
  • Coalition Town Halls
  • Climate art competition in partnership with CID's Global Empowerment Meeting
  • Three caucuses, including a DEI Caucus
  • Mailing list

Mission, in their words

During the week leading up to Earth Day (which falls on April 22nd), the Harvard Climate Coalition will collaborate with various student groups on campus to organize a series of events and actions aimed at educating and engaging the Harvard community on the intersections between the climate crisis and our own lived experiences. We will highlight how the climate crisis exacerbates other forms of social injustice, impacts our career choices, sparks forms of creative expression, and more. Our goal is to promote a holistic understanding of the climate crisis and to encourage everyone to take action to address this urgent global issue.If you would like to get involved please join the #earth-week channel on our slack or send us an email to [email removed] hyped for INTERSECTIONAL EARTH WEEK!
